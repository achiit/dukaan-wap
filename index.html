Our Story

My father's kadhai, still lit

My father, Shri Hari Prasad Gupta, started this shop in 1994 with one kadhai and a wooden counter. I was twelve and my job was to stir the sugar syrup. I still remember the smell of kesar on Diwali night, with a queue going all the way to the clock tower.

Today my son helps at the counter, but the recipe has not changed. We use the same ghee supplier, the same early start and the same rule: if a batch is not right, it does not go in the box.

  • 100% pure desi ghee, never vanaspati
  • Made fresh daily, nothing kept overnight
  • Taste before you buy, always
  • Home delivery within Sitapur town
  • Sugar-light options for elders
